Project Brief: Customer Portal Redesign

Overview:
The customer portal redesign aims to modernize the self-service experience for our top-tier accounts. The current portal was launched five years ago and no longer meets accessibility standards or mobile expectations.

Goals:
- Increase self-service resolution rate from 58% to 75% by end of Q3.
- Reduce average support ticket volume by 20% within six months of launch.
- Achieve WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility compliance.

Scope:
The redesign covers the dashboard, invoice history, ticket submission, and knowledge base sections. The billing engine and authentication system are out of scope and will remain unchanged.

Timeline:
- Discovery and requirements validation: complete by March 15.
- Design review with stakeholders: scheduled for April 2.
- Implementation phase: April through July.
- User acceptance testing: must be completed by July 25.
- Launch: targeted for August 1, pending sign-off from the product owner.

Key stakeholders:
- Product owner: responsible for final scope decisions.
- Engineering lead: will deliver weekly progress reports.
- Design team: needs to prepare the component library by April 10.
- Support team: should review the new ticket flow before UAT.

Risks:
The team has not yet validated the new invoice view with real users. We should schedule a usability test with five customers before implementation begins. The accessibility audit will need to be scheduled early to avoid delays.

Next steps:
1. The project manager will send the consolidated requirements document to all stakeholders by Friday.
2. Engineering must prepare a technical spike for the SSO integration.
3. Design needs to follow up on the empty-state illustrations.
4. We need to confirm the budget for the accessibility audit by end of month.
